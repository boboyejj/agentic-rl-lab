# Pilot review: 41 items from 20 abstracts

Mark each item: **U** = useful as a review criterion for your work, **N** = not useful, **E** = useful after edit (write the edit).
Exit criterion: at least 60 % U+E.

## Rubric items (criteria)

- [ ] `r0000` (performance_benchmarking, medium) **Stability under continuous operation**: Does the work test stability and recyclability of the metal-containing zeolite in continuous operation, not only initial activity?  
  _Source: mintova 2020, "Emphasis on the Properties of Metal‐Containing Zeolites Operating Outside the Co" doi:10.1002/anie.202005498. Quote: "Particular attention will be given to studies that explore the stability and recyclability of metal‐containing zeolite catalysts operating in continuous modes."_

  _Source: gounder 2021, "Dynamic Interconversion of Metal Active Site Ensembles in Zeolite Catalysis" doi:10.1146/annurev-chembioeng-092120-010920. Quote: "active site ensembles often restructure in response to their external environment and even during steady-state catalytic turnover"_
